James Furniture Rebellion: Support

Need help with James Furniture Rebellion? Email support.utegaardgames@gmail.com and tell us your device and what happened.

How do I play?

Tap TAP TO START, hold your device at a comfortable angle and tap RECENTER & GO. Tilt left or right to move the nail gun, and tap anywhere to fire.

Tilt steering feels off.

Recalibrate from the calibration screen, or adjust sensitivity, dead zone and smoothing in Settings.

Can I play without tilting?

Yes. On the calibration screen, tap "Use touch controls instead". Then drag to move and quick-tap to fire.

How do I unlock Boss Rush?

Beat wave 20 in the main game. Boss Rush then opens from the title screen.

Do I need Game Center or an internet connection?

No. The game plays fully offline. Game Center is optional and is used only for leaderboards and achievements.

Does the game have ads or collect data?

No ads, no accounts and no data collection. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected. See our privacy policy.
