James Furniture Rebellion: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy covers the James Furniture Rebellion app for iPhone and iPad, made by Utegaard Games.

We collect no data

James Furniture Rebellion does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. The game plays fully offline.

App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

Your high scores, the on-device top-10 table and your settings (such as tilt calibration, sensitivity and haptics) are saved only on your device. We never receive them.

Motion sensor

The game reads your device's motion sensor only while you play, so you can steer by tilting. That data is used in the moment and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you sign in, Apple's Game Center handles the main-game and Boss Rush leaderboards and the achievements under Apple's own privacy policy. We don't receive or keep any Game Center data ourselves. The game works the same without Game Center.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
